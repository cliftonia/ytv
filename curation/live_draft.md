Every channel's `why` in live_draft.json says what placed it. Only Pluto's guide carries programme genres; the Samsung, Plex and Roku guides give titles and lengths, so for those channels a guide counts through known show titles, films against series, and the service's own channel group. A channel with no guide of its own may be placed by what another service's same-named channel airs (`via`), but keeps `guide: none`.
